# Mumbai Monsoon Patterns

Mumbai, located on India's west coast, receives some of the heaviest monsoon
rainfall among major Indian cities due to its exposure to the Arabian Sea
branch of the Southwest Monsoon and the orographic lift caused by the Western
Ghats.

The city's monsoon season typically runs from June through September, with
peak rainfall intensity usually observed in July. Mumbai is prone to sudden,
intense rainfall events that can exceed 200mm in a single day, often leading
to urban flooding due to a combination of high tide timing, drainage capacity
constraints, and rapid urbanization reducing natural water absorption.

Historical extreme events, such as the July 2005 Mumbai floods, highlight the
city's vulnerability to concentrated heavy rainfall episodes, underscoring the
importance of accurate short-term precipitation forecasting for disaster
preparedness.
