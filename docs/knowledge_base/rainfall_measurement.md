# Rainfall Measurement and Classification

Rainfall is typically measured in millimeters (mm) of water accumulated over
a specific time period, most commonly 24 hours, using rain gauges or
increasingly, remote sensing and radar-based estimation.

The India Meteorological Department (IMD) classifies daily rainfall intensity
into categories: no rain (0mm), very light rain (0.1-2.4mm), light rain
(2.5-15.5mm), moderate rain (15.6-64.4mm), heavy rain (64.5-115.5mm), very
heavy rain (115.6-204.4mm), and extremely heavy rain (above 204.5mm).

These classifications help in issuing weather warnings and assessing flood
risk. Extreme rainfall events, while less frequent, disproportionately
contribute to infrastructure damage and loss of life, making their prediction
a high priority despite being inherently harder to forecast accurately than
typical rainfall days.
